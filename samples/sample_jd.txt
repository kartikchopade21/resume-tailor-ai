Machine Learning Engineer — Applied AI
Northgate Analytics · Bengaluru (Hybrid)

About the role
You will build and ship LLM-powered features that sit in front of real customers.
You will own retrieval pipelines end to end, from ingestion through evaluation,
and work with product engineers to get models into production.

Responsibilities
- Design and deploy RAG pipelines over internal knowledge bases
- Fine-tune and evaluate large language models for domain-specific tasks
- Build and maintain prompt engineering workflows and eval harnesses
- Deploy inference services on AWS with Docker and CI/CD
- Monitor model quality and latency in production
- Partner with backend engineers to expose models through REST APIs

Required qualifications
- 2+ years building machine learning systems in Python
- Hands-on experience with LLMs and prompt engineering
- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Experience building RAG pipelines with a vector database (Pinecone, FAISS, or pgvector)
- Strong SQL and data pipeline skills
- Experience deploying services with Docker on AWS

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with LangChain or LlamaIndex
- Familiarity with MLOps tooling and model monitoring
- Experience with FastAPI
- Exposure to Kubernetes
- Contributions to open-source ML projects

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
